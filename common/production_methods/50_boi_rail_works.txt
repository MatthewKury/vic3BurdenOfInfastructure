#  more skilled than roads. Tiers follow vanilla's railway technologies.

#  fewer were built than in vanilla, and steel and engines fell with them.
#  Same day, iteration 2: 1.5 railway levels' output (30/38/53/68).
#  Prices: iron 40, steel 50.
# ================================================================
