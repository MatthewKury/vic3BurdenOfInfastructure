# Plan for the 3 Nov 2026 request

Executes [the request](00_request_3_Nov_2026.md). Results land in `docs/reports/` and the module plans.

## Decisions (2026-11-03)

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Housing's infrastructure use in 1836 | Middle: Slums 3.5, Workers' Rows 3, Town Houses 2.5, Carriage Villas 4 |
| AI redevelopment | Test removing a single level. If it works, a hidden event replaces old levels in short states. If not, new builds only |
| Population floor | Stays at 100,000. May move to 125,000 during balance if infrastructure still doesn't bite, and is reported as a rule change |
| Cap on works per state | None for now. Cheaper housing construction carries replacement first |
| Light and Heat bonus | Wood +10%, Oil +30%, Electricity +60% of the building's housing output |
| Century-run telemetry | Drop `boitm_boi.2` from century runs, keep the other lines, resume from an autosave on a crash |
| Balance aim | Bite without collapse: GDP within about 5% of vanilla, civil wars and radicals near vanilla, housing supply just under demand |
| Git | Checkpoint on a branch, then one commit per stage and per balance pass. Nothing pushed |

Defaults taken from the proposals, unless they prove wrong: old building types stay buildable until a law bans them; Residential Standards bans new Slums, Workers' Rows and Tenement Blocks; Temporary Shelter gets no Light and Heat group; company housing on workplaces keeps its own tiers; slum pollution (+50) stays out of this work.

## Stages

1. **Engine probes.** One short headless fixture run answers what the design depends on: does an input shortage cut `state_infrastructure_add`, does `possible` block a build, does `required_input_goods` work on oil and automobiles, can one level be removed by script, and is infrastructure released when it goes.
2. **Road and Rail Works.** New Rail Works, retuned Road and Harbour Works, a financing group per building, law text, AI event choosing between the three.
3. **Housing tiers.** All 15 buildings, Light and Heat groups, new construction and infrastructure costs, seed and generator updates, tests, placeholder icons, AI replacement if stage 1 allows.
4. **Telemetry.** Levels by building and fuel, rail and works spending, automobile, oil, electricity and services markets, state pollution where safe, a century-run profile.
5. **Short runs.** Run, write down the takeaways, correct, repeat. Each pass is logged in `docs/reports/balance_3_Nov_2026/BALANCE_LOG.md`.
6. **Two century runs and a report** in `docs/reports/century_3_Nov_2026/REPORT.md`.

Lessons go into `VIC3_MODDING_LESSONS.md` in the session they are learned.
